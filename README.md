# SWYNEX Interactive Dashboard

## 📊 FMCG Retail Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed as part of my Data Analyst Internship at SWYNEX Technologies.

The dashboard transforms cleaned FMCG retail transaction data into an interactive visual report that helps analyze revenue, transactions, product categories, countries, payment methods, and purchasing quantities.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To build a professional and interactive dashboard using Power BI that communicates the key findings from the analyzed FMCG retail dataset through KPIs, charts, and interactive filters.

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dashboard is based on the cleaned FMCG retail transaction dataset prepared during the previous internship tasks.

### Dataset Details

- **Total transactions:** 1,480
- **Total revenue:** ₹4,087,666.92
- **Total quantity sold:** 3,656
- **Product categories:** Electronics, Groceries, Fashion, Home
- **Countries:** Nigeria, United States, Ghana, United Kingdom, Turkey
- **Payment methods:** Card, Cash, Transfer
- **Time period:** 2024–2026

## 📌 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

The dashboard provides four key performance indicators:

- Total Revenue
- Total Transactions
- Average Revenue per Transaction
- Total Quantity Sold

### Visualizations

- Revenue by Country
- Revenue by Product Category
- Monthly Revenue Trend
- Revenue by Payment Method
- Revenue by Quantity

### Interactive Filters

Users can filter the dashboard using:

- Country
- Product Category
- Payment Method
- Year

The visuals update dynamically based on the selected filters.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Electronics** generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- **Nigeria** recorded the highest total revenue among the countries.
- **Card payments** contributed the highest total revenue and had the highest number of transactions.
- Average revenue increased as the quantity purchased increased.
- The monthly revenue trend shows noticeable variations across the analyzed period.
- Higher-value transactions were generally associated with higher quantities and unit prices.

---

## 🎨 Dashboard Design

The dashboard uses a dark navy and blue theme to provide a clean, modern, and professional visual experience.

The layout was designed to present information in a clear flow:

**KPIs → Revenue Distribution → Time Trend → Payment & Quantity Analysis**
